> A meeting is for thinking together, not for reporting. Come to make a decision or get unstuck.

## Before

Send (or bring) a short update the day before: what you tried, what you found, where you're stuck, and the **one or two things** you most want to discuss. Even three bullets is enough — it lets me prepare and doubles the value of our time.

## During

Lead with the question or decision, not a chronological recap. Show the evidence (a plot, a number, a snippet). It's completely fine — encouraged — to say "I'm stuck" and to disagree. We're debugging ideas, not defending them.

## After

Write down what we decided and the next concrete step while it's fresh. Own the follow-ups.

## Kinds of meetings

- **1:1s** — your project, your agenda; I follow your lead.
- **Group meeting** — share works-in-progress, practice talks, read together.
- **Ad hoc** — when you're blocked, a 15-minute unblock beats two silent days.
