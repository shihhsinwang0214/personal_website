> Expectations work when they go both ways. Here's mine of you and yours of me.

## What I expect from you

- **Ownership.** Treat your project as yours. Drive it; don't wait to be pushed.
- **Honesty.** About results, about credit, and about what you don't understand yet.
- **Communication.** Share progress *and* problems early. "I'm stuck" on Monday is worth ten silent days.
- **Effort that matches the goal.** I care about steady, real progress and thoughtful work — not hours logged or seats warmed.
- **Respect for people's time.** Come to meetings prepared; do your reading; keep shared spaces and code usable for others.

## What you can expect from me

- Clear direction early, and room to find your own later.
- Timely, specific feedback and real attention when you're stuck.
- Fair credit and honest advocacy for your goals.
- A safe environment to be wrong, ask "basic" questions, and disagree with me.

## On rhythm and balance

Research is a marathon. Sustainable, focused effort beats bursts followed by burnout. Take real breaks, protect your health, and tell me if the load is off — that's a normal, expected conversation, not a failure.

## When expectations aren't met

We talk about it directly and early, on either side. Most issues are small if caught early and painful only if left silent.

## In short

You own the work and communicate honestly; I give direction, feedback, and fair credit. Both of us keep it sustainable.
