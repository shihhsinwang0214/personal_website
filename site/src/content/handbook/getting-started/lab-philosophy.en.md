> We're here to understand things deeply and build tools that are honest, reliable, and useful — and to grow as people while we do it.

Everything in this handbook flows from a few beliefs. If you internalize these, most day-to-day decisions answer themselves.

## Culture over rules

Rules tell you what to do; culture tells you what to do when there's no rule. We'd rather share principles you can apply in new situations than hand you a rulebook. When in doubt, ask: *what would a careful, curious, honest researcher do here?*

## Understanding over output

A paper is a byproduct of understanding something real. We optimize for genuine understanding — the kind you can explain simply, defend under questioning, and build on next year — not for the count of results. Deep beats fast.

## Independent thinking over task completion

The goal is not for you to complete my tasks; it's for you to become someone who finds and solves the right problems. Early on I'll point at problems; over time you'll bring them to me. Finishing a task is good; owning a direction is the point.

## The four things we care about

- **Curiosity** — chase the *why*; let questions pull you.
- **Ownership** — treat the project as yours; nobody should have to chase you.
- **Integrity** — honest with data, with credit, and with yourself about what you don't know.
- **Persistence** — push past the first wall; most good results live on the other side of a hard week.

## Friendly and serious at once

High standards and a warm, supportive environment are not in tension — they reinforce each other. We're kind to people and tough on ideas.

## In short

Understand deeply, think independently, act with integrity, and keep going. The rest of the handbook is just this, applied.
