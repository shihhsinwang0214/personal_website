> Expectations only work when they go both ways. This page is a contract we sign together, not a list of terms I hand you.

## What I promise you

- Although the choice is mostly still yours, I'll be strict about quality — assessing a topic's or idea's potential early, so you don't waste time on something with no value.
- I will tell you what I really think, including things you don't want to hear — possibly tactful, but never vague.
- I will not be unreachable when you need me. Come find me when you're stuck — don't wait for the next meeting. And when you don't need me, you're welcome to cancel early. :)
- Your growth comes before my output. If a project is good for producing a paper but not for your development, we will talk seriously about how to arrange things.
- I will do my best to protect your time, so you don't have to deal with admin and chores that aren't yours.
- When you leave, you should be able to independently choose problems and independently judge whether a piece of research — a result — is credible. That matters more than how many papers you published.

## What I expect from you

- **Communicate good and bad news proactively.** Whether the news is good or bad, let me know on Slack. I might reply a bit late, but I'll keep it in mind.
- **Own your results.** Before showing a result to others, pick it apart yourself first.
- **Respect shared resources.** Compute, data, other people's time.
- **Help those more junior than you.** Everyone here has been helped by someone more senior — including me.
- **Take care of your body and mind.** The pace of this field can make all-nighters look like a virtue, but they aren't. Sustained, steady output beats two or three months of relentless burning. And when you want a break, take it properly — I won't message you (I take breaks too).

## When the agreement isn't kept

We talk about it directly and early, on either side. Most issues are still small if caught early, and painful only if left silent. If you feel I haven't done something I said I would, tell me directly — that conversation won't damage our relationship; avoiding it will.
