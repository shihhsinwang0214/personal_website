> The following are my suggestions for new members.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Read the documents in this section carefully; look through the failure log; have a 30-minute chat with every member |
| Week 2 | Set up your environment and reproduce an existing result, explaining it in your own understanding. The goal is not to make something new, but to walk the full path once |
| Week 3 | Pick a small problem or direction you find interesting and run [Level 1](/personal_website/handbook/daily-practice) |
| Week 4 | Present at group meeting — **including the parts you messed up**. This is how we grow |

Don't worry — nobody expects you to produce some amazing new result in your first month.
