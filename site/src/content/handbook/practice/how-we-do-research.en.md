> For students and postdocs who may join — or have already joined — the lab (and for reference for people who want to collaborate with us).

This section lays out some of our research style: what I believe, how the day-to-day works, what I promise students, and what I expect students to do. It's split across the pages below — read them in order, or just take what you need right now:

- [Six Things I Believe](/personal_website/handbook/research-principles) — trying and debugging, toy examples, problem selection, fewer and deeper, intuitive thinking, speaking up.
- [How We Discuss](/personal_website/handbook/how-we-discuss) — challenge ideas not people, "I don't know" beats bluffing, newcomers speak up.
- [How the Day-to-Day Works](/personal_website/handbook/daily-practice) — group meetings, one-on-ones, the debugging ladder, writing one page before you start, open source.
- [Authorship and Research Contribution](/personal_website/handbook/authorship) — actual contribution is the sole principle for author ordering.
- [Promises and Expectations](/personal_website/handbook/promises-and-expectations) — what I promise you, and what I expect from you.
- [Your First Month](/personal_website/handbook/first-month) — a suggested four weeks after joining.
- [For Collaborators](/personal_website/handbook/collaborators) — how cross-field collaboration works, and the timeline realities.

If you're considering applying, this section is also what question 10 of the [Research Reflection Questionnaire](/personal_website/handbook/research-reflection-questionnaire) asks you to respond to.

## Honestly, who this place may not suit

- People who want very explicit instructions at every step. I'll give you a starting point and context, but not the detailed route.
- People who can't work in uncertainty for three months. Research often goes long stretches with no visible progress (even if our toy examples give a little bit of joy).
- People who see admitting mistakes as weakness — our entire way of operating is built on the opposite assumption.

If reading these makes you quite uneasy, that doesn't mean you don't fit at all — most people feel that way at first.

You can choose another lab, or talk with me before joining.
