> Over-communicate progress and problems; under-communicate noise. Silence is the expensive option.

## Principles

- **Default to sharing.** A short "here's where I am / here's where I'm stuck" beats a polished silence. Nobody should have to wonder how a project is going.
- **Raise problems early.** Bad news doesn't improve with age. "I'm blocked" on day one saves a week.
- **Match the channel to the message.** Quick questions and coordination in chat; thinking, decisions, and anything you'll want to find later in writing; hard or sensitive things in person.
- **Respect focus.** Async by default; not everything needs an instant reply. Protect deep-work time — yours and others'.

## Response norms

We're responsive but not on-call. A rough expectation: same-day-ish for chat during working hours, longer for deep feedback. If something is urgent, say so and how urgent.

## Being remote / across time zones

Write things down so work doesn't depend on being online at the same moment. Prefer clear written updates over meetings when a meeting isn't needed.
