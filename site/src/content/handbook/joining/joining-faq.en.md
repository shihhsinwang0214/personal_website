> If your question isn't here, that's a good reason to email — but read this first.

**Do I need to have published before?** No. I care about how you think and whether you follow through, not about a publication count.

**Do I need a specific background?** No single background is required, but the research here is fairly math-heavy. You don't need to already know the tools we use — what matters is being willing to learn them and able to. To calibrate what that means, see the concrete examples in [Who Should Apply](/personal_website/handbook/who-should-apply).

**Is the reflection questionnaire required?** Yes — it's part of the application; please attach your answers to your first email. But there are no correct answers and nothing is graded; it's the starting point of our first conversation. See the [Research Reflection Questionnaire](/personal_website/handbook/research-reflection-questionnaire).

**Can I do a rotation / short project first?** Often yes. A small, time-boxed project is a great way for both of us to see how we work together.

**Do you take undergraduate researchers / interns?** It depends on capacity and fit. Reach out with something specific and I'll tell you honestly.

**What if my interests are slightly different from the lab's?** Adjacent interests are welcome and often lead to the best projects. Very distant ones may be a better fit elsewhere — I'll say so kindly.

**How much guidance will I get?** A lot early on, deliberately less over time — the goal is to grow your independence. See [Mentoring Philosophy](/personal_website/handbook/mentoring-philosophy).

**What are your expectations around hours / working style?** I care about progress and ownership, not hours logged. See [Expectations](/personal_website/handbook/expectations).
