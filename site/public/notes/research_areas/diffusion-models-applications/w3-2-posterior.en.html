At small t, often only a few nearby candidates have large weights in this empirical toy. Increasing t spreads the weights and directs the estimate toward their weighted mean, possibly between modes rather than on a likely clean observation. This is the squared-error optimum: the conditional expectation, not a guaranteed recovery of the particular source.
