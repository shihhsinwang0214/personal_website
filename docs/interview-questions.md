# Interview Questions — private reference

Not for the website. A short, plain set of questions to draw on during an interview or first conversation with a prospective student. Screen applications the normal way (CV, transcript, a short statement, prior projects); use these in person to understand *how someone thinks*.

How to use them: pick 3–5, not all. There are no correct answers — you're listening for reasoning, honesty, and self-awareness, not the "right" response. Let silence do some work; follow up with "why?" and "can you give a concrete example?"

Distilled from the longer reflection questionnaire (kept in the repo but hidden from the site).

---

## Curiosity
- **EN:** Tell me about something you couldn't stop thinking about that wasn't for a class or a job. What pulled you in?
- **ZH:** 跟我說一件你停不下來去想的事，而它不是為了課或工作。是什麼把你吸進去的？
- *Listen for:* intrinsic interest vs. outcome-driven; how far they chase something for its own sake.

## Tolerance of uncertainty
- **EN:** Describe a time you had to keep going on something important without knowing whether it would work out. What did you do with the not-knowing?
- **ZH:** 描述一次你得繼續做一件重要的事，卻不知道它會不會成功。你怎麼跟那種「不知道」相處？
- *Listen for:* honest relationship with ambiguity; concrete coping strategies rather than "I stayed positive."

## Resilience
- **EN:** Tell me about a real setback — not the tidy lesson afterward, but what actually happened in the days right after.
- **ZH:** 跟我說一次真實的挫折——不是事後整齊的教訓，而是那之後幾天實際發生了什麼。
- *Listen for:* how they metabolize disappointment in real time; whether failure stops or redirects them.

## Decision-making
- **EN:** Finish a solid result on time, or keep pushing a riskier idea that might be much better but might fail? Walk me through how you'd actually decide.
- **ZH:** 準時完成一個紮實的成果，還是繼續推一個風險較高、可能好很多但也可能失敗的想法？帶我走過你實際上會怎麼決定。
- *Listen for:* visible reasoning under a real trade-off; what they weigh — not which side they pick.

## Learning style / meta-cognition
- **EN:** When you want to truly understand something new, what do you actually do first, second, third? How do you know when you "get it"?
- **ZH:** 當你想真正搞懂一個新東西，你實際上第一步、第二步、第三步會做什麼？你怎麼知道自己「懂了」？
- *Listen for:* self-knowledge about their own process; a real method vs. a generic answer.

## Independence vs. collaboration
- **EN:** What energizes you more — deep solo work, or figuring things out with other people? Be honest about the mix.
- **ZH:** 什麼更讓你有能量——一個人深入地做，還是跟別人一起把事情想清楚？誠實說出那個比例。
- *Listen for:* where they draw energy; any honest ratio is fine — you're checking project/style fit.

## Collaboration under friction
- **EN:** A teammate's part of a shared project isn't working and it's slowing everyone down. What do you do — and what do you make sure *not* to do?
- **ZH:** 團隊中有人負責的部分兜不起來、拖慢了大家。你會做什麼——以及你會確保自己*不*做什麼？
- *Listen for:* balance between getting the work done and treating people well; the "what not to do" half.

## Changing your mind
- **EN:** What's something you used to believe fairly strongly that you've since changed your mind about? What changed it?
- **ZH:** 有沒有一件你曾經相當堅信、後來改變了想法的事？是什麼改變了它？
- *Listen for:* whether they update on evidence/experience; the mechanism of change, not the topic.

## Motivation
- **EN:** If your best work were never publicly recognized, but you alone knew it was true and useful — would that change what you'd work on?
- **ZH:** 如果你最好的工作永遠不會被公開肯定，但只有你知道它是真的、有用的——這會改變你想做的事嗎？
- *Listen for:* honest mix of intrinsic vs. recognition-driven motivation; self-honesty over performed humility.

## Communication
- **EN:** Explain something you understand well to someone who knows nothing about it — pick anything. (Listening for how you make it clear, not the topic.)
- **ZH:** 向一個一無所知的人解釋一件你懂得很透的事——什麼都可以。（聽的是你如何講清楚，不是主題。）
- *Listen for:* structure, assumptions about the listener, use of intuition/analogy/example.

## Math explanation (announced on the site — expect prepared answers)
- **EN:** Pick one mathematical tool you know well — SVD, the Jacobian, conditional expectation, change of variables, a simple ODE, or anything else — and teach it to me like I'm a classmate who hasn't seen it.
- **ZH:** 挑一個你最熟的數學工具——SVD、Jacobian、條件期望值、變數變換、簡單的 ODE，或任何其他的——像教一個沒看過它的同學那樣教我。
- *Listen for:* whether they convey the idea (geometry/intuition) or recite the formula; how they respond to a follow-up outside the prepared path ("what breaks if we drop this assumption?", "give me a second example"). Since this question is public on Who Should Apply, the follow-ups carry most of the signal.

---

### Notes for yourself
- Keep it conversational, not an interrogation. The goal is mutual understanding of fit, not scoring.
- Watch for reasoning you didn't expect — divergent, authentic answers are the good outcome.
- If an answer is generic ("I'm a hard worker," "failure motivates me"), gently ask for a concrete example; that's where the signal is.
